# Provisional NaCl water-activity convention

The ideal osmotic water-activity calculation counts two dissolved particles per
NaCl formula unit and one per already represented Na or Cl ion. Thus equivalent
NaCl and Na/Cl compositions give identical activity under the existing phi=1
exponential relation. This assumes fully dissolved, fully dissociated NaCl; the
convention requires scientific review before merging. It does not validate
nonideal solution activities or change the packaged kappa or molar masses.
The rule is specific to NaCl: it does not infer dissociation of arbitrary custom
formula units. Already separated ions are counted once, and mixed encodings are
additive. Existing coefficient-based solute classification is retained. Activity
requires positive water mass; the existing dry nucleation-rate short circuit is
preserved rather than defining dry-particle solution activity.
See Rose et al. (2008), Appendix A4:
https://acp.copernicus.org/articles/8/1153/2008/acp-8-1153-2008.pdf
